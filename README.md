# Tangency

Design custom road blocks for **Trackmania 2020** and build them straight into the game.

Draw a road shape, choose straights, turns, slopes and banks, and Tangency makes the blocks and puts them in your Items folder, ready to place in the editor.

## Download

**[Download TangencySetup.exe](https://github.com/camplatt/Tangency/releases/latest/download/TangencySetup.exe)** (Windows 10 and 11)

1. Run `TangencySetup.exe`. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because Tangency is new and not yet signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Open Tangency from the Start menu or your desktop.
3. The first time, Tangency finds your Trackmania folders and offers to install **NadeoImporter** (Nadeo's free tool that turns models into blocks).

No account and no administrator rights needed. Tangency updates itself: an **Update** button appears in the app when a new version is out.

## Where things go

| | |
|---|---|
| Your blocks, projects and road shapes | `Documents\Tangency` |
| Blocks you build | `Documents\Trackmania\Items\Tangency` |
| The 10 newest blocks | `Items\Tangency\Y_Recent` in the game's item browser |

Uninstalling Tangency (Windows Settings → Apps) keeps both folders.
